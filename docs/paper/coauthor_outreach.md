# Finding an academic co-author: plan and message template

## Who to look for

Do not write to famous names first. Write to people who publish measurable, mechanistic work on the questions VIDA instruments, and who would gain from a reproducible platform:

1. **Computational consciousness / theories of consciousness.** Groups working on integrated information measures, global workspace models, or indicator-based assessments of AI (for example the authors of the 2023 "indicator properties" report and groups that compute Φ-like quantities on artificial systems). VIDA offers them a system where every indicator can be measured and ablated.
2. **Metacognition and confidence.** Researchers on metacognitive sensitivity (type-2 signal detection). Criterion 4's result (functional metacognition without privileged access) is directly in their territory.
3. **Artificial life and agent-based social simulation.** The ALIFE community, and groups doing reputation, deception and trust in multi-agent systems. The deception ledger and lineage instruments are of independent interest to them.
4. **Philosophy of mind with an empirical bent.** Someone who works on personal identity, self-models or the hard problem and is willing to be the methodological conscience of the paper (what the criteria can and cannot show).

Where to find them: recent arXiv (cs.AI, q-bio.NC) and PsyArXiv preprints on machine consciousness indicators; ALIFE and Artificial Life journal programme committees; the Association for the Scientific Study of Consciousness (ASSC) membership; Mexican and Latin American groups in cognitive science (UNAM, CINVESTAV, UDLAP) who can supervise locally.

## What to offer

- A working, tested, open platform with six criteria already instrumented and a negative result on integration.
- A preprint draft ready for their critique and co-authorship.
- Full reproducibility: configurations, seeds, event digests.
- Willingness to be wrong: two caught errors are reported in the paper; a co-author's job includes finding the third.

## What to ask for

- A critical reading of `docs/paper/vida_preprint.md` (one hour).
- If they find it sound: joint revision toward a venue (ALIFE 2027, *Artificial Life*, *Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness*, or *Neuroscience of Consciousness*), with arXiv first.
- If they find it unsound: the specific objection, in writing. That alone is worth the email.

## Message template (English)

Subject: Reproducible simulator with six operational consciousness criteria measured; seeking a critical co-author

Dear Dr. [Name],

I am an undergraduate researcher at the Universidad Tecnológica Metropolitana de Aguascalientes, Mexico. Over the past months I built VIDA, an open, seed-reproducible multi-agent simulator in which agents with episodic, semantic and reflective memory, a learned self-model, relationships, deception and full life cycles live in a world that is the sole authority over outcomes.

I used it to measure six operational criteria drawn from the consciousness-science literature: self-model predictive advantage, causal necessity of the self-model, reported states tracking hidden variables, metacognitive sensitivity, communicated uncertainty, and informational integration (a behavioural Φ proxy). Five hold in their functional reading, each with a mechanism that fully explains the result. The sixth fails: Φ is zero across 8,424 decisions per seed and 23 of 24 agents. I also report one artefact I caught (an action-dependent feature inflated the self-model's information by two orders of magnitude) and one metric I discarded.

I am writing because your work on [specific paper or topic] is exactly the perspective this needs. I am looking for a co-author who will read the preprint critically and tell me where it is wrong; if it survives, I would like to revise it with you for [venue]. The draft, code and raw results are at [repository URL]. The preprint is about 4,000 words.

I understand you are busy. Even a short note saying which criterion you find least convincing would be valuable to me.

With thanks,
